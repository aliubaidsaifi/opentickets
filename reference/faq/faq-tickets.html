TICKETS & PURCHASING

Back to FAQ Categories
To book tickets, please locate the event you would like to attend, select the number of tickets you wish to purchase, and add them to your cart.

If the event has allocated seating, please select your preferred seat(s) and the ticket category/price option you would like to purchase before adding them to your cart.

Once you are ready, click Checkout, enter your details, and then click Purchase. You will be redirected to our secure online payment page to complete your transaction.

Once payment has been successfully processed, your tickets will be sent to the email address provided during checkout.
If you are able to select a ticket and add it to your cart, tickets are still available for purchase. Please note that we do not display the number of tickets remaining for an event.
