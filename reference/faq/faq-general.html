Once your payment has been completed successfully, you will receive one email containing your order confirmation and ticket options.

Scroll down in the email until you see the following buttons:
Add to Google Wallet
Add to Apple Wallet
📱 View Tickets Online
📄 Download PDF
🌐 Download HTML

You can use any of these options to access your tickets:
View Tickets Online – Opens your tickets in your web browser where you can view and scan them.
Download PDF – Downloads a PDF copy of your tickets to your device.
Download HTML – Downloads an HTML version of your tickets that can be viewed in your browser.
Add to Google Wallet or Add to Apple Wallet – Saves your tickets directly to your mobile wallet for easy access on the day of the event.

If you have purchased multiple tickets, all of your tickets will be available through the same email. When viewing your tickets online, you can scroll left or right to view each individual ticket.
If you are unable to locate your email, please check your Spam, Junk, or Promotions folder.
